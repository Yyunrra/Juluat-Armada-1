Welcome to Mellow Craft Studio

Crocheting is one of my favorite hobbies because it allows me to create something beautiful and cute using my hands. Using only a crochet hook, yarn, and my imagination I can make everything, just like Krim Stix "Imagination mo and limit" HAHAHAHHAHAHAHA🤣

What started as a simple hobby eventually became a part of my life, Mellow Craft Studio, my small cramped space for making and sharing my crafts. I enjoy experimenting with different patterns, colors, designs, as well as buying tons of yarns that I know I would never use or touch again. For me crocheting is more than just looping yarns, it is my way to relax, create something new, and maybe a backup plan when I go broke.

My Crochet Must Haves:

  • Crochet hooks
  • Yarns
  • Scissors
  • Stitch markers
  • Yarn Needle
  • Fiberfill (for stuffing plushies)
  • Safety eyes (for plushies)
  • Glue gun or B 7000 glue
