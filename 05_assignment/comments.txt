This assignment took me around 30 minutes only. I used the past examples as a guide on making each element of the form.
While making the the form, I noticed that I wouldn't be able to copy the exact form, that's why I used <br> and <strong> to replicate the spacing and uniformity of each section title/label. However, at the submit button section, I wasn't able to replicate the gap between the text box and the submit button. When I placed the <br> below the text box, it didn't show, however when I placed it above the submit button, instead of creating a gap between the textfield and the button, it created a padding above the word submit. So what I did instead was to remove the <button> element sorrounding the submit button and leave the <input type="submit"> as it was. This allowed me to replicate the spacing successfully.
